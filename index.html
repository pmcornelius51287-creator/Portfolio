Additional Team Findings

The full team assessment also identified outdated WordPress (6.8.3) with vulnerable plugins, exposed /wp-json/wp/v2/users endpoint, verbose error messaging enabling login enumeration, no account lockout policy, and an expired SSL certificate.

Social Engineering Assessment

Designed and executed two GoPhish phishing campaigns targeting 19 employees using OSINT-driven pretexts: a CareFirst BlueCross benefits enrollment impersonation (exploiting open enrollment timing) and a local coffee shop holiday promotion. Both campaigns were intercepted by email security systems.

Email Breach Audit

Audited 19 company emails via HaveIBeenPwned. Found 5 associated with known breaches, 3 with compromised passwords. Recommended password policy enforcement, security awareness training, and MFA.
